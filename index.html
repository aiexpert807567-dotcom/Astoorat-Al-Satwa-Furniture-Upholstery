About Us

Craftsmanship since 1981.

Astoorat Al Satwa Furniture Upholstery L.L.C. has been based in Dubai since 1981, working on furniture, vehicle and boat upholstery for the local community. Today the workshop continues that same hands-on approach — fabric, foam, leather and repair work carried out for each piece that comes through the door.
